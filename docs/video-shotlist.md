## 2. Synthetic Demo (0:30 - 2:00)

*   **Timestamp:** 0:30 - 0:50
    *   **On-Screen Action:** Start the "Synthetic" Replay. Show the map with the vehicle moving normally under "TRUSTED GNSS".
    *   **Voiceover:** "We'll begin with a synthetic replay to demonstrate the system's core behavior. Here, the vehicle has trusted GNSS."
*   **Timestamp:** 0:50 - 1:15
    *   **On-Screen Action:** The replay enters "DEGRADED" and then a total GNSS "BLACKOUT" phase. Focus on the map showing the diverging tracks: INS drifting away versus our fused estimate staying on the road.
    *   **Voiceover:** "As we enter a degraded state and then a total blackout, watch the trajectories. The raw inertial navigation (INS) rapidly drifts. However, our fused estimate remains constrained and continues along the expected path."
*   **Timestamp:** 1:15 - 1:30
    *   **On-Screen Action:** Open the Replay Lab. Trigger a "Pothole" fault injection. Observe the system's response (e.g., covariance inflation or error state adjustment).
    *   **Voiceover:** "Using the Replay Lab, we inject a pothole fault. The system detects the context change and inflates the covariance, ensuring the disturbance doesn't corrupt the long-term heading."
*   **Timestamp:** 1:30 - 1:45
    *   **On-Screen Action:** Use the Event Ledger to seek back and forth in the replay.
    *   **Voiceover:** "We can seamlessly seek through the event ledger to inspect the exact moment GNSS was lost and how the filter responded."
*   **Timestamp:** 1:45 - 2:00
    *   **On-Screen Action:** Open the Evidence view. Show the metrics table and click the JSON/CSV export buttons. Then quickly flip to the Architecture/System view.
    *   **Voiceover:** "Our Evidence view provides transparent metrics, which can be exported to JSON or CSV. The architecture is modular, separating the Python training pipeline from the C++ mechanization core."
